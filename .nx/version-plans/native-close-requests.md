A close the user starts and the app does not answer no longer leaves the toolkit and React out of step. Adwaita dialogs without `onClosed`, `onCloseAttempt` or `onResponse`, and JSX tab pages whose `AdwTabView` has no `close-page` handler, stay open; `showSidebar`, `showContent`, `open` on `AdwBottomSheet` and `AdwTabOverview`, and `searchModeEnabled` are controlled when given. In development, GTKX warns once per element type with the handler to add. See the [upgrade guide](https://gtkx.dev/v2/guide/upgrading-to-2#check-the-behavior-changes).

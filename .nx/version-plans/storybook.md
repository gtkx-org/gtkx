The Storybook explorer supports `argTypes` `mapping` and `if`, hides stories tagged `!dev`, shows fractional number arguments and groups stories by title path. `gtkx storybook` reopens the current story after a restart, scrolls tall stories, skips `.mdx` files, accepts story globs in sibling workspace packages, logs story reloads and explains when `@gtkx/storybook` is missing. `composeStories` types stories that declare their own args.
